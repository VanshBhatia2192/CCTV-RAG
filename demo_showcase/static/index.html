Spatial-Temporal Graph Convolutional Architecture (ST-GCN)

Human gait is modeled as a non-Euclidean graph \( G = (V, E) \) where \( V = 17 \) joints and \( E \) represents anatomical skeletal bones.

Spatial Graph Convolution Equation:
X^(l+1) = σ( Σ_{k} D^(-1/2) · (A_k ⊙ M_k) · D^(-1/2) · X^(l) · W_k )

Where:
• A_k : 17x17 Anatomical bone adjacency matrix with self-loops (A + I)
• M_k : Learnable edge importance attention mask
• D : Degree matrix D_{ii} = Σ_j A_{ij}
• W_k : 1x1 Spatial convolution kernel weights
• Temporal Conv : 1D convolution over T frames (kernel size 7) with residual skips
